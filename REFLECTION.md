# End-of-course reflection (300 words is a ceiling, not a target)

Re-read your notes and trap logs from Homeworks 1, 2 and 3 before you write. Graders look for four specifics, not
for polish.

1. **Of the three notes you wrote — HW1, HW2, HW3 — which is your strongest, and why?** Quote one sentence from it.

2. **One step of the loop** (observe → locate → hypothesize → inspect → change → verify → explain) **that you changed,
   or deliberately kept, across the three homeworks.** Give a concrete example from your work, and what it cost or
   saved you. Cutting a step and paying for it is one honest answer; keeping a step when it was tempting to skip it
   is another.

3. **Which class of failure was slowest for you** — grain, NULLs, joins, JSON, cleaning, or checks? What is your
   concrete plan for getting faster at it?

4. **How did your use of AI change between Homework 1 and Homework 3?** Give one example. If you did not use it, say
   how you verified your work instead, with one example: that is an equally good answer.
